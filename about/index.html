Our story

Jelcos started near Meditrina Hospital in Ayathil, Kollam — founded by Mr. Prakash, who saw families struggling to find reliable care after discharge. What began as a small nursing team is now one of Kerala's most-reviewed home healthcare providers.

Our Kalluvathukkal branch extends that same standard to Parippally, Chathanoor, Paravoor, Varkala, Kallambalam, Attingal and Kazhakoottam — so quality care isn't limited to the city.

NRI families are a big part of our story: daily updates, photo reports and a coordinator abroad families can call any time give them peace of mind from thousands of miles away.

Verified, trained staff
